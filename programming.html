What works

The battle is the part that works. I can fight, knock enemies out and capture them on the spot, with stronger enemies harder to win over.

My army splits into up to four squads that I shout orders at: follow, hold, charge or retreat. Hold Space and the camera pulls back so I can command everyone. Fights end in a win, a loss or a retreat, and the result carries over.

It's all coloured squares for now, on purpose. The world map is next.
